## Decision states

- `LOCK`: eligible for carry-forward only after independent validation and complete evidence.
- `ABSTAIN`: insufficient or ambiguous evidence; never promoted automatically.
- `REJECT`: boundary or policy violation; never injected into the next cycle as a premise.
- `BLOCKED`: required input, contract, schema, or execution evidence is missing.
- `UNRESOLVED`: question remains open without an authoritative answer.

A prior-cycle result may enter cycle n+1 only when its record is immutable, source-grounded, independently verified, and explicitly approved for carry-forward. A locked result is not automatically a universal premise.
