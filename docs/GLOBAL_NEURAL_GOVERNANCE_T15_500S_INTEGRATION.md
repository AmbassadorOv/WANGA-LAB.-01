## Purpose
Integrate two complementary public-accountability and sovereignty methods into AI231 / WANGA, the World Government Institute's planned public services, and the citizen interface.

**The citizen is not the turkey. The system must answer for the recipe:** public institutions and contractual arrangements must make obligations, beneficiaries, evidence, and transferred costs reviewable.

## 1. T15 — 15-Second Accountability / Thanksgiving Turkey
T15 is a rapid accountability and alerting frame, not a claim that a complex investigation can be completed in fifteen seconds. It makes obligations and their effects visible and routes them into human review.

Six operational stages:
1. Collect the ingredients: public debt, contracts, guarantees, budgets, resource rights, and social/environmental obligations.
2. Examine the contract: authority, consent, terms, performance, conflicts of interest, and possible coercion.
3. Identify imbalance: unmet obligations, hidden liabilities, resource extraction, and cost externalization.
4. Prepare remedies: restitution, restructuring, compensation, revenue sharing, or mediation.
5. Put options before the public: local, national, regional, or international deliberation, according to lawful competence.
6. Record and review: commitments, execution status, transparency, correction, and appeal.

### T15 Debt-Service Alert
A contextual alert protocol assesses payment capacity, debt sustainability, liquidity, resilience to economic shocks, and transfer of burdens to other states or populations. No universal debt-to-GDP threshold is asserted. No automated finding of culpability, insolvency, illegality, or aggression is made.

## 2. 500-Second Sovereignty Test
A 500-second (8m20s) controlled simulation window for testing dependencies and institutional resilience. It is a tabletop/computational exercise, not a validated crisis-prediction threshold.

Scenario families:
- Supply Disruption: critical supply-chain interruption.
- Platform Dependency: reliance on external platforms, cloud, identity, or infrastructure.
- Public Budget Shock: fiscal stress and service continuity.
- Trust Breakdown: loss of public trust and information integrity.

Each exercise records critical services exposed, evidence and provenance of dependencies, affected groups and rights impacts, available alternatives and recovery times, safeguards and responsible human decision-makers, unresolved uncertainties, and corrective actions.

## 3. Citizen-facing public service
The planned Institute portal should let a person submit a proposal, complaint, evidence, or supporting document; read the proposed Second International Civil Constitution and its version history; join public deliberation and track a proposal; participate in clearly labelled simulations or consultations; request correction or appeal an analysis; and inspect public commitments and remediation status.

Citizen participation is not conditional on buying Logic Coin. A token or contribution record must not purchase additional voting power or political influence. Pilot consultations are not official elections and do not replace lawful institutions.

The planned state and citizen service is intended to be free to recipients at this stage. This does not mean cost-free operation: funding, sustainability, agreements, data governance, and oversight must be separately defined.

## 4. System architecture mapping
| Layer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| AI231 / WANGA dynamic-logic and evidence services | ingest, normalize, trace sources, test consistency, expose uncertainty, abstain when evidence is insufficient |
| T15 service | obligations register, contract review workflow, cost-transfer analysis, alert routing, remedy options |
| 500-second simulator | scenario orchestration, dependency map, service-impact analysis, recovery options, exercise report |
| Institute governance layer | rights checks, role separation, case management, independent review, appeal and public reporting |
| Citizen interface | proposals, evidence submissions, deliberation, consultations, status tracking and appeals |
| AI Companies Cooperative | specialist engineering, security, data, integration, simulation and operations under bounded charters |

## 5. Minimum data model
Case, Obligation, Contract, Party, Beneficiary, EvidenceItem, CostBearer, PublicService, Dependency, Scenario, Impact, RemedyOption, Deliberation, DecisionRecord, Appeal, AuditEvent.

Every material claim should carry source locator, capture time, version, author/issuer, integrity digest where available, uncertainty description, and review status. Hashes and timestamps support integrity and chronology; they do not by themselves prove truth or legal validity.

## 6. Human rights and governance boundaries
- The system produces analysis, alerts, simulation results, and options; it does not exercise sovereign or judicial authority.
- Human decision-makers remain accountable under applicable law.
- An institution that is party to a dispute cannot be its sole reviewer.
- Provide privacy, accessibility, security, anti-coercion safeguards, audit logs, correction, appeal, and a stop mechanism.
- Separate public-service funding from commercial development and IP licensing.
- The proposed constitution has no binding legal force absent lawful adoption by competent authorities.
- Logic Coin remains research/design work; not presented as legal tender, a tradable asset, or a means of purchasing civic influence.

## 7. Acceptance tests for an MVP
1. Replay a T15 case from source references and reproduce each stage.
2. Show a contract's authority, consent, terms, performance evidence, and unresolved questions separately.
3. Identify a transferred cost and show evidence and alternative explanations.
4. Run all four 500-second scenario families with declared assumptions and deterministic replay.
5. Produce an affected-services and affected-groups report with uncertainty.
6. Demonstrate independent review, correction, appeal, and a full audit trail.
7. Demonstrate that payment/contribution status cannot alter civic participation weight.
8. Demonstrate privacy controls, role-based access, retention policy, and safe stop.
9. Label outputs VERIFIED, ASSESSMENT, HYPOTHESIS, or NOT_YET_VERIFIED according to actual evidence.

## 8. Delivery sequence
Stage 0: source inventory, legal/IP mapping, data definitions, threat model.  
Stage 1: T15 case schema and manual review workflow.  
Stage 2: 500-second scenario runner and dependency graph.  
Stage 3: citizen portal for proposals, evidence, deliberation, status, and appeals.  
Stage 4: AI231/WANGA integration, provenance, abstention, replay, and audit.  
Stage 5: controlled pilot, independent evaluation, security and rights assessment.

Do not add unpriced scope to the existing ₪300–450M planning range without a task-based estimate and duplicate-cost check.
